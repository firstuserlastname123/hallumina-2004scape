Hallumina's personal bot controller uses the rs2b0t project as its primary
interface and script-management reference:
https://github.com/rs2b2t/rs2b0t

The metadata registry, defineBot helper, settings schema and generated parameter
form/searchable library patterns are selectively adapted from its MIT-licensed
implementation. Its client, network protocol, game catalogs, external loader,
account management and activity scripts are not included.

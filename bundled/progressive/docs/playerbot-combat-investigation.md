# Phase 1.2 combat investigation on Progressive r254

This report records the pre-fix investigation at `5a8b103`. The implementation, focused regressions and paired population results are documented in [Melee combat reliability](playerbot-melee-combat.md).

**Recommend PR #5 focus on the lifecycle of one melee encounter, from choosing an eligible target through looting and choosing the next one.** A bot can fight and earn native rewards today. It can also abandon a target it has already attacked to open an unrelated gate, abandon Combat because it equipped the required weapon, or spend an entire watchdog interval waiting for a nonexistent target. Each failure has a small reproduction on the merged engine.

The investigation used `dev` at `5a8b103403498fe891379ebbfe607b21eafe98c5`, including PRs #1–#4. Measurements below use the bundled map, NPC configs, scripts and collision in a disposable assembly. The VM evidence available here is the supplied summary. The corrected side of the previous paired population runs measured `1a0ba61`, before the last PR #4 gate-verification change. ## What the VM counters establish

The VM sustained 100 active bots beyond 7,500 ticks. The logger counts events before filtering or rate limiting. An actor can contribute to several counters in one encounter. The summary supplies neither encounter histories nor denominators such as attempted fights or time spent on Combat. Managed fixtures cover persistence. Human play and live restart persistence in this VM session remain unverified.

| VM observation | Interpretation and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 314 unreachable targets | Melee logs this when its approach budget expires and the NPC origin is outside a one-tile square. It suppresses that NPC for 100 playtime ticks in the task instance. At Lumbridge this fired for a chicken that had already received a native attack interaction. At Edgeville four different NPCs timed out. Switching targets worked there, yet the bot earned zero melee XP in the observation window. |

At 22 the planner deferred Combat for 100 ticks for “requirements unavailable.” Reset moved the same sword back into the backpack and Agility took over. No attack occurred. | The walk state equips the weapon through the normal held-item operation. The next eligibility check uses `hasItem`, which counts only the backpack. Reset makes the same failure possible on a later assignment. Planner checks also use backpack ownership. Count a suitable *usable* melee weapon across the two possession states at both entry points. |
| **Edgeville, blocked face and legal alternative** (`decisions`) | From (3101,3511,0), the selector chose `man2` at (3100,3511). The player could not operate from there, and native entity routing returned no approach. | **Edgeville during NPC movement** (`edge`) | By world tick 200 there had been nine barrier attempts, four verified openings, four obstacle failures, two walking-stuck recoveries, and four distinct target timeouts. A later tick can replace a useful approach. The separate static spawn test above isolates one selection error. |
| **Two kills and six real pickups** (`world`) | NPCs 5430 and 5426 despawned at ticks 100 and 174 after native combat. Both produced `no_attack_timeout` eight ticks later. The backpack received two bones, two raw chickens and 20 feathers through native pickups. Both kills used Attack. A single post-kill timeout in the preceding scenario still allowed collection and a second fight. |

Flee currently steers toward the training location and can finish within its radius while still in danger. |

It moved on **297 of 300 ticks**, most recently on tick 300 at (2859,3504), with 113 observed travel-progress increments. Three source findings affect the proposed fix:

- A successful wider search on the second scan miss is discarded by an unconditional return. The claim registry uses a coordinate/type fallback because the r254 NPC's slot is `nid`, while the claim code checks `index`. A roaming NPC can change that fallback key. The native competition/rejection rules still apply. Exercise two bots and a moving target before attributing VM timeouts to claim collisions. [Search, claims and scan](../engine/src/engine/bot/tasks/CombatTask.ts)
- `setCombatStyle` only writes `com_mode`. The r254 style button in the assembled `content/scripts/skill_combat/scripts/player/player_attackstyles.rs2` also recalculates the derived combat stats. Bind rotation to the applicable weapon interface and confirm the next hit advances the intended skill through RuneScript. [Bot action](../engine/src/engine/bot/BotAction.ts)
- Combat also manually removes big bones and awards Prayer XP, and calls teleport helpers for dungeon entry, exit and desperate walking recovery. The shared watchdog's old “teleported” log message is stale, but those separate calls are real. Use the ordinary bury operation and native supported access, or defer. A dungeon step needs its own correct entrance and requirements. Audit the paths used by Combat without enabling generic recovery teleports. ## Proposed PR #5 and focused acceptance tests

| Rank | Change | Impact and complexity | Acceptance test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Correct current HP reads and melee weapon ownership. Use actual worn protection, usable weapon, edible inventory and the candidate's r254 combat data when choosing between a dangerous and an easier target. When hurt, eat owned food or withdraw along a legal route away from the threat. | High / small for the data reads and ownership, moderate for retreat and suitability. Pair the two fixes so that resolving the sword deferral cannot send the unprepared trainee straight into the guard fight. | Replay the 2/10 case with and without one owned food, and the guard case with novice and prepared loadouts. A qualifying worn weapon survives the next task tick. Food is consumed by its native operation. Show a safe alternative or bounded retreat before another attack. |
| 2 | Retain a selected target through approach and combat. Rank a small shortlist of available, attackable NPCs by validated melee approach cost. Prefer an already operable target. Check the native attack option and player-visible range at click time. Allow a different candidate or a relevant native gate when necessary. | High / moderate. Bounds the search work and removes detours while preserving doors, requirements and native competition. | Replay both Edgeville candidates and the chicken gate. Reject a closest-possible endpoint that fails `reachedEntity`. Cover a larger footprint, a moving target with two bots, a legitimately closed gate, and a locked approach with an alternative. A native attack with zero damage must not cancel itself to open an unrelated door. Keep claim and negative-cache lifetimes bounded. |
| 3 | Handle target loss, delayed death, empty/blocked loot and reacquisition explicitly. Release the claim once, perform legal pickups, resume the scan, and select the next valid style using native controls. Remove task-awarded melee XP and the big-bone award. | High / small to moderate. Restores productive time and makes XP interpretable. | Repeat the two-kill fixture at 1×. Observe attack, damage, despawn, drop ownership, actual inventory receipt and the next native attack. Test a competing kill and empty loot. Require zero task-originated melee rewards, normal Prayer from burying, and correct native XP for the next style. |
| 4 | Keep the Combat-only timeout out of measured travel. Within encounters retain a finite no-progress budget. Replace the Combat-specific stuck/dungeon shortcut with a verified native route or a deferral. | High for long training trips / small for the timeout, moderate for access handling. | Repeat fit and poor journeys, plus the existing stationary, retarget, locked-door and gateway fixtures. Moving toward a stable route may continue beyond 300 ticks. Stationary wandering must still expire. No new access flags, payment, items or position jumps. |

I would include those four changes within melee Combat and its direct planner/interaction callers, sharing the corrected HP read with ranged/magic. Keep broad ranged targeting, global pathfinding, Agility scheduling, shopping and new dungeon routes for separate changes. Unsupported routes can defer. No save migration, XP correction to existing characters, or content change is needed.

Run the focused fixtures first. For each attempted encounter record bot, tick, NPC identity, target/standing tile, native reach, attack dispatch, XP source, despawn, owned pickup, new target and deferral reason. Measure ticks to first attack, time to the next productive action, repeated failures on the same target or barrier, and actual native participation per bot. If the stationary fixtures pass but combat oscillation remains unaccounted for, replay a few affected profiles with recent tiles and target history. A paired population run becomes useful after the local before/after evidence demonstrates a behavioral improvement.

## Secondary findings

- **Agility.** In the two historical corrected seeds, 44 and 50 of the 52 progression bots saw Agility, with 52,242 total Agility-task ticks including travel. A task completion requires ten laps in one task instance. Extending the Gnome fixture with its normal balanced planner counted seven laps by tick 581 and 559.0 Agility XP by that point. At tick 650 the periodic rescan selected Thieve and reset the lap count to zero. The existing fixture independently verifies a native lap through floors 0, 1 and 2. This demonstrates how productive training can yield zero task completions. Population-level attribution still needs per-bot arrivals, native obstacle/lap XP, lap counts and exit reasons. Historical access constraints and travel can also reduce participation. [Agility task](../engine/src/engine/bot/tasks/AgilityTask.ts) · [Planner rescan](../engine/src/engine/bot/BotPlayer.ts)
- **Obstacles and mining.** A known locked door genuinely refuses passage, times out and is suppressed. The Zanaris door fixture verifies a native crossing from the reachable operating side. An independent historical observer could miss passage through a temporary invisible wall. In the new chicken trace an obstacle failure and an actual feather receipt both occurred on tick 124. At Edgeville, four successful openings still produced no melee XP over the observation. Correlate each attempt with the next arrival or fight before changing gate policy. The previously traced miner resumed earning XP after switching rocks. There is no evidence here for changing normal mining. [Door and course fixtures](../engine/tests/fixtures/bot-environment.ts) · [Prior movement traces](playerbot-gate-review-results.json)
- **ShopTrip.** The paired total fell from 534 completions to 397, while all 52 progression characters earned native XP in every run and their combined native XP rose from 65,892.2 to 96,796.9. Some individuals earned less in the corrected runs, 13 of 52 in seed 254 and 18 of 52 in seed 255. The historical records do not link an intended purchase or missing tool to that individual's next task. There is no demonstrated aggregate progression loss attributable to shopping. To test individual starvation, trace one required item from planner request through payment, inventory receipt and first successful use. ShopTrip can also mark an unknown shop done, and its buy helper transfers items after a fixed wait, so the completion counter alone is a poor measure of native purchases. ## Reproduction and verification

Use a fresh disposable checkout with the probe present and the project's existing Progressive assembly procedure. From the assembled packed engine with dependencies installed, run the modes independently. Each opens its own temporary SQLite working directory and loads new in-memory characters. The fixture weapon and skill levels model initial conditions and never enter character saves. [Observation tool](../engine/tools/bot/investigate-combat.ts) · [Assembly instructions](playerbot-reliability.md)

```sh
for mode in world edge geared decisions travel travel-fit agility; do
    node --import tsx tools/bot/investigate-combat.ts "$mode" || exit 1
done
```

The three encounter modes run at most 200 full world cycles with seed 254, admit one headless player after 20 warm-up ticks and observe actual NPC movement, combat and pickups. The other modes isolate decisions or advance the same player movement, script and planner phases as the existing fixtures. The empty-loot test explicitly enters the post-loss state. The travel and agility tests do not model roaming NPC attacks en route. Accelerated runs advance activity timers by 600 ms per game tick. These are bounded reproductions on selected inputs. They give no estimate of how often an individual VM bot enters each case.

The existing suites passed **32/32** on this assembly, comprising 22 reliability and native bot tests, five persistence tests and five build-integrity tests including the parent case. The clean pack build ran with all three verification switches and emitted the two missing-model warnings for IDs 434 and 2925. The integrity suite also rejected altered NPC, object and interface content. TypeScript still reports the 11 previously documented protocol diagnostics and none in the probe. The freshly generated script pack and ondemand ZIP differ in SHA-256 from the historical run. The loc pack and supplementary collision CSV match. The historical population totals above came from the earlier packs.

node --import tsx --test tests/bot-reliability.test.ts tests/bot-runtime.test.ts
